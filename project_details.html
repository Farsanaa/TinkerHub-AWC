Loopit - Student-to-Student Marketplace

Date: 31st July 2026

Ansar Women's College Hackathon 2026 Won 1st Prize

Loopit is a student-to-student campus marketplace designed to make buying and selling second-hand academic essentials simple, affordable, and sustainable. Many students have items like textbooks, lab coats, calculators, stationery, and other college essentials that they no longer need. At the same time, junior students often have to buy these items at full price. Loopit bridges this gap by providing a dedicated platform where students within the same college can buy and sell items safely.
