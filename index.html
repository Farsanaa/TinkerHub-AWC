A Space to Build, Learn, & Share

Tinkerhub Ansar Women’s College (Tinkerhub AWC) is a student-led technology and creative community at Ansar Women’s College, created to help students explore, learn, build, and share beyond the boundaries of traditional classrooms. Our community brings together students who are curious about technology, design, creativity, innovation, problem-solving, and emerging digital skills. Whether someone is a complete beginner or already has experience, Tinkerhub AWC provides a space where everyone can learn at their own pace, experiment with ideas, collaborate with others, and turn those ideas into something real.

  • check_circle Weekly hands-on coding and maker hackathons
  • check_circle Dedicated mentorship from senior campus developers
  • check_circle Open-source contribution sprints and support
